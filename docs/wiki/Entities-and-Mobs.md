## 👑 Emerald Titan (Boss Encounter)

The **Emerald Titan** represents the ultimate combat challenge in Emerald Essentials:

### Boss Mechanics
- **Physical Might**: Stands at 4.6 blocks tall with high base attack damage and natural knockback immunity.
- **Fire Immunity**: Completely immune to fire and lava damage, preventing easy cheese strategies in subterranean depths.
- **Minion Reinforcements**: Periodically summons waves of **Emerald Scuttlers** to swarm and distract challengers.
- **Enrage Phase (Below 50% HP)**:
  - When the Titan's health drops below 50%, its movement and attack speed increase significantly.
  - Attacks produce heavier shockwaves, requiring players to utilize ranged weaponry or agile mobility (such as *Slime Lotus* bounces or *Emerald Dagger* stealth).

### Unique Rewards
- **Emerald Core (`emerald_core`)**:
  - An iridescent crystalline core dropped only upon defeating the Emerald Titan.
  - Cannot be obtained through standard crafting or transmutation.
  - Essential component for endgame progression artifacts:
    - **Emerald Crown**
    - **Emerald Staff**
    - **Emerald Keystone**

## 🪲 Emerald Scuttler

- **Projectile Reflectance**: Its thick emerald carapace can bounce incoming arrows and ranged projectiles, forcing players to engage in close-quarters melee combat or flank with backstabs.
- **Stealth Synergy Target**: Ideal opponent against which to deploy the **Emerald Dagger**'s backstab critical multiplier.
- **Spawn Eggs**: Available in the creative inventory under `emerald_scuttler_spawn_egg`.

## 🔮 Amethyst Scarab

- Attuned to the harmonic frequencies of **Amethyst Blocks** and **Amethyst Resonators**.
- Emits soft crystalline chimes when moving and produces faint purple particle trails.

## 🍄 Mushroom Bup & 🍃 Mantabu

- Non-hostile ambient creatures designed to enrich the underground world.
- **Mushroom Bup**: Small bouncy fungal creature that wanders cavern floors.
- **Mantabu**: Peaceful gliding entity that traverses cavern airways and deep caverns.
