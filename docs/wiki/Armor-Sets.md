# Armor Sets

Emerald Essentials introduces two full armor sets: **Emerald Armor** (high enchantability and protection) and **Ruby Armor** (endgame durability, armor toughness, and knockback resistance).

## 💎 Emerald Armor Set

The **Emerald Armor Set** (`emerald_helmet`, `emerald_chestplate`, `emerald_leggings`, `emerald_boots`) provides balanced protection superior to Iron and competitive with Diamond:
- **Total Defense**: 19 Armor Points (9.5 shields).
- **Armor Toughness**: +1.0 per piece.
- **Extreme Enchantability**: Rated at **25**, it easily rolls level 30 enchantments such as Protection IV, Unbreaking III, and Mending, making it one of the easiest sets to perfect at an enchanting table.
- **Repair Material**: Standard `minecraft:emerald`.

---

## 🔴 Ruby Armor Set

The **Ruby Armor Set** (`ruby_helmet`, `ruby_chestplate`, `ruby_leggings`, `ruby_boots`) represents elite progression between Diamond and Netherite:
- **Total Defense**: 20 Armor Points (10 full shields).
- **Armor Toughness**: +2.0 per piece (+8.0 total set), dramatically reducing high-damage attacks from bosses and creepers.
- **Inherent Knockback Resistance**: Each piece provides +0.05 knockback resistance (+0.20 for the complete set), preventing the player from being knocked off cliffs or into lava.
- **Durability**: Significantly exceeds standard Diamond gear.
- **Repair Material**: `emerald_essentials:ruby`.

---

## 🎨 Armor Trim Compatibility

Both Emerald and Ruby armor sets are fully registered in the vanilla Minecraft 1.21.1 `#trimmable_armor` item tag.

- Fully compatible with all 16 vanilla **Smithing Templates** (Coast, Dune, Eye, Host, Raiser, Rib, Sentry, Shaper, Silence, Snout, Spire, Tide, Vex, Ward, Wayfinder, Wild, and Bolt).
- Compatible with all material trim ingots (Amethyst, Copper, Diamond, Emerald, Gold, Iron, Lapis, Netherite, Quartz, and Redstone).
- Render textures utilize standard vanilla equipment model layer mappings (`equipment/humanoid/` and `equipment/humanoid_leggings/`).
