# Welcome to the Emerald Essentials Wiki

**Emerald Essentials** transforms vanilla emeralds, rubies, and amethysts into a deep, cohesive progression system with high-tier tools, versatile armor sets, magical botany, subterranean biomes, and unique endgame artifacts for **Minecraft 1.21.1**.

Built on a unified modern multi-loader architecture supporting both **Fabric** and **NeoForge**.

## 🌟 Key Pillars

1. **Meaningful Emerald Utility**: Elevates emeralds beyond mere trading currency into viable mid-to-high tier gear with exceptional enchantability.
2. **Ruby Tier Progression**: Discovered deep underground or through gemstone transmutation, Ruby equipment bridges the gap between Emerald and Netherite.
3. **Harmonic Amethyst Technology**: Magical artifacts such as the **Amethyst Resonator**, **Amethyst Lens**, and **Amethyst Altar** provide sonic utility, mob detection, and transmutation.
4. **Living Botanical Magic**: 13 rare flowers scattered across diverse biomes offer ambient light, particles, status effects upon contact, and ingredient potential.
5. **Multi-Loader Parity**: 100% identical gameplay, mechanics, and visual fidelity across Fabric and NeoForge.

## 📦 Compatibility & Requirements

- **Minecraft Version**: `1.21.1`
- **Java**: `Java 21` or higher
- **Supported Mod Loaders**:
  - **Fabric**: Requires [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/) `0.16.0+` & [Fabric API](https://curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fabric-api)
  - **NeoForge**: Requires [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net/) `21.1.0+`
