# Equipment and Tools

Emerald Essentials expands your arsenal with two complete material tiers (**Emerald** and **Ruby**), specialized weapon archetypes like **Daggers**, custom combat gear (**Bow** & **Shield**), and ancient magical artifacts.

## 🗡️ Weapons & Tools Breakdown

### Standard Toolset
Both **Emerald** and **Ruby** feature the standard suite of tools:
- **Sword**: Fast, precise sweeping slashes.
- **Pickaxe**: High-efficiency excavation capable of breaking Obsidian and deep ores.
- **Axe**: High single-hit burst damage and shield-disabling capability.
- **Shovel**: Rapid dirt and gravel clearing.
- **Hoe**: Tilling and scythe-harvesting crops with extended reach.

### 🗡️ Daggers (`emerald_dagger`, `ruby_dagger`)
Daggers are lightweight, high-speed melee weapons designed for agile combat and stealth gameplay:
- **Attack Speed**: Ultra-fast attack cooldown (1.8+ attacks/sec).
- **Stealth Backstab Synergy**:
  - Striking an opponent from behind (outside their line of sight) deals **2.5x critical backstab damage**.
  - If the player is under the effect of **Invisibility** (e.g., from `shadow_orchid` or potions), the strike deals **3.5x damage** and does not immediately break stealth.

### 🏹 Emerald Bow (`emerald_bow`)
- Crafted with Emeralds and Starchords.
- Features higher arrow velocity, tighter accuracy grouping, and increased base durability over vanilla wood bows.
- Supported by custom pull-stage animations (`emerald_bow_pulling_0`, `_1`, `_2`).

### 🛡️ Emerald Shield (`emerald_shield`)
- High-durability defensive shield adorned with emerald reinforcement.
- Features dedicated active blocking rendering (`emerald_shield_blocking`).
- Negates 100% of frontal projectile and melee physical damage with increased durability resistance against explosive impacts.
