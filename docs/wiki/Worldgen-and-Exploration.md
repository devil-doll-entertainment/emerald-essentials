# Worldgen and Exploration

Emerald Essentials introduces vibrant subterranean additions to the Overworld, expanding mining and exploration with a dedicated cave biome, rare ore veins, and ancient ceremonial altars.

---

## 🏞️ Emerald Caverns Biome

The **Emerald Caverns** (`emerald_caverns`) is a specialized subterranean cave biome that generates deep beneath mountain and plateau biomes (between Y=-16 and Y=-58).

### Biome Characteristics
- **Visuals**: Dense crystalline glow with emerald-green cavern fog and ambient crystalline sparkles.
- **Flora**: Abundant colonies of **Illusional Flowers**, **Echo Violets**, and **Vitallia** growing along the cavern floor and mossy ledges.
- **Mineral Abundance**: High natural concentration of exposed Emerald and Ruby veins embedded directly in stone and deepslate walls.

---

## 💎 Mineral Generation

### 1. Ruby Ore (`ruby_ore` & `deepslate_ruby_ore`)
- **Generation Range**: 
  - Stone Layer: Generates from Y=16 down to Y=0 in small clusters of 2–4 blocks.
  - Deepslate Layer: Generates from Y=0 down to Y=-64 with peak density at Y=-48.
- **Harvest Requirements**: Requires an **Iron Pickaxe** or better.
- **Drops**: Yields 1 `emerald_essentials:ruby` (affected by the **Fortune** enchantment) or the ore block itself when mined with **Silk Touch**.

---

## 🏛️ Amethyst Altar (`amethyst_altar`)

The **Amethyst Altar** is a mystical interactive block found naturally within rare cavern grottos or craftable using smooth basalt, amethysts, and an emerald keystone.

### Utility & Functions
- **Catalytic Infusion**: Placing raw gems or mundane items onto the altar while channeling harmonic tools (such as the *Amethyst Resonator*) unlocks alchemical transmutations.
- **Gem Transmutation**: Converts emeralds into rubies or enchants standard gemstones into crystalline power sources.
- **Light & Aesthetic**: Emits a soft purple glow (Light Level 9) and resonant chimes when activated by players.
