# Crafting and Recipes

Emerald Essentials offers intuitive recipes that follow vanilla crafting logic, while introducing specialized transmutation and endgame infusion pathways.

---

## 🔨 Equipment Recipes

### 1. Standard Emerald & Ruby Tools
Crafted in the standard vanilla 3x3 crafting grid layout:
- **Sword**: 2 Gems + 1 Stick
- **Pickaxe**: 3 Gems + 2 Sticks
- **Axe**: 3 Gems + 2 Sticks
- **Shovel**: 1 Gem + 2 Sticks
- **Hoe**: 2 Gems + 2 Sticks

### 2. Daggers (`emerald_dagger`, `ruby_dagger`)
- **Layout**: 1 Gem + 1 Stick placed vertically in a crafting grid.
- **Cost**: Inexpensive early/mid-game weapon, ideal for fast combat and stealth builds.

### 3. Bow & Shield
- **Emerald Bow**: 3 Emeralds + 3 Strings in standard bow formation.
- **Emerald Shield**: 1 Emerald surrounded by 6 Wood Planks in the standard shield shield-pattern.

### 4. Armor Sets
Crafted in the traditional armor patterns using **Emeralds** or **Rubies**:
- **Helmet**: 5 Gems (horseshoe shape)
- **Chestplate**: 8 Gems (full jacket)
- **Leggings**: 7 Gems (pants outline)
- **Boots**: 4 Gems (two side boots)

## 🔮 Magical & Endgame Artifacts

### Amethyst Resonator
- **Grid Pattern**: 
  - Top: Amethyst Shard
  - Center: Emerald
  - Bottom: Iron Ingot

### Amethyst Lens
- **Grid Pattern**: 
  - Center: Amethyst Shard
  - Sides: Gold Nuggets / Glass Panes

### Emerald Mirror
- **Grid Pattern**:
  - Center: Ender Pearl
  - Borders: 4 Emeralds + 4 Gold Ingots

### Emerald Whetstone
- **Grid Pattern**:
  - Top: Emerald
  - Bottom: Smooth Stone / Flint

### Emerald Crown
- **Grid Pattern**:
  - 5 Emeralds across the top and side corners + 3 Gold Ingots forming a regal band.

### Emerald Apple
- **Grid Pattern**:
  - Center: Golden Apple (or standard Apple)
  - Surrounding: 8 Emeralds

### Enchanted Emerald
- **Infusion / Crafting**:
  - 4 Amethyst Shards + 4 Emeralds surrounding a Nether Star or Diamond Block at an **Amethyst Altar**.

## 🔥 Smelting & Blasting

| Source Block | Furnace / Blast Furnace Output | Experience |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| `ruby_ore` | 1 `ruby` | 1.0 XP |
| `deepslate_ruby_ore` | 1 `ruby` | 1.0 XP |

> [!TIP]
> **Blast Furnaces** smelt Ruby ores at double the speed of traditional furnaces.
